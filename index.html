About Me

I am Mohamed Diaa, a Graphic Designer, a graduate of the Higher Institute of Computers and Information – Department of Management Information Systems, class of 2023.

Driven by my strong passion for design and visual content creation, I started my professional journey by completing an intensive two-month Graphic Design course, during which I earned a Certified Graphic Design Certificate from Intro Academy & ADucation.

Through this program, I mastered the fundamentals of design and developed strong skills in Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Dimension.

Afterward, I joined a 5-month practical training program at Al Faris Marketer Agency, where I developed my expertise in designing advertising campaigns and managing social media visually.

I currently work as a freelance graphic designer, collaborating with several brands in Saudi Arabia (such as Nkht balh for Sweets, Tea and More Café, Zawit Zad Burger Restaurant), in addition to continuously providing design services to various local businesses in Egypt, including restaurants, cafés, a medical laboratory, a dental clinic, a car accessories company, and a perfume shop.

I also use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Nano Banana, Kriya Ai, and Free Peak Generate Ai to enhance my designs and keep up with the latest market trends.

This diversity of experience has given me strong skills in analyzing the needs of each business and delivering design solutions that enhance the visual identity and present the product in the best possible way.
